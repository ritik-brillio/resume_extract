45% Weight

Technical Skills Match

Direct lexical and semantic matching of required technical skills, languages, libraries, tools, and platforms across the entire resume.

  • Searches across Skills, Projects, and Experience
  • ATS sigmoidal curve: matching ≥50% core terms scales to 75-85%
  • Filters out non-technical action verbs (e.g. using, experience)
35% Weight

Applied Experience & Responsibilities

Evaluates whether the candidate has executed the core responsibilities and workflows in actual work history.

  • Higher depth multiplier for work experience (1.0) and projects (0.95)
  • Recency scoring: recent and current roles score higher
  • Excludes generic preamble and non-substantive text
10% Weight

Applied Project Justification

Validates if required skills are actively applied and proven in real projects or work history rather than just listed in a raw keyword bullet.

  • Grants bonus when skills are substantiated by project bullets
  • Separates theoretical skill claims from hands-on execution
10% Weight

Years of Experience (YoE) Fit

Compares candidate's verified relevant months of experience against the JD minimum experience requirement (e.g. 2–5 years).

  • Measures domain-relevant experience (e.g. QA testing months for QA roles)
  • Full credit (100%) when meeting or exceeding the minimum years
